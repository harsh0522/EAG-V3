Here are 5 reputable institutes in Bangalore offering CNC/VMC training, ranging from industry-backed centers to government-run vocational programs:

1. IMTMA (Indian Machine Tool Manufacturers' Association) Technology Centre: An industry-backed facility in Bangalore offering comprehensive, systematic training from basic to advanced levels, including CNC programming, maintenance, and CAD/CAM using actual production machinery.

2. GTTC (Government Tool Room and Training Centre): A government-backed initiative with multiple Bangalore locations, highly regarded for structured long-term vocational training and short-term skill development in CNC machining and precision engineering.

3. Nettur Technical Training Foundation (NTTF): A well-established Bangalore institution offering diploma and certificate programs in tool and die making, which integrate significant hands-on CNC/VMC operation and programming curriculum.

4. Maria CNC Institute: Known for intensive, skill-based, hands-on training focusing on industry readiness, including VMC and CNC lathe programming, G-code, and live machining.

5. HI-TECH CAD/CAM/CNC Technologies: Offers a comprehensive 6-month Advanced Diploma covering CNC/VMC operating, programming, and tooling, catering to a broad range of students from 10th-grade graduates to engineering professionals.
